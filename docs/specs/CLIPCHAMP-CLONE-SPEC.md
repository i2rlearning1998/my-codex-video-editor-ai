# Clipchamp clone spec (V-series): shell, timeline, Scene outliner, FX panels, transitions

Revision 3, 2026-10-10. Owner direction: the whole editor must look and behave like Clipchamp (same layout, timeline, right panels, FX previews), with a Blender-style collection outliner in the LEFT "Scene" category. No bug may be left from the lists below.

Sources (all reviewed frame by frame, 2026-10-10): owner's bug recording "Bugs of video project" (573 s), Clipchamp timeline recording (381 s), Blender collections recording (143 s), owner's PDF "New bugs" (3 pages), Clipchamp filters/effects recording (303 s), Clipchamp transitions recording (60 s). SEEN = visible in frames. INFERRED = deduced. NOT SEEN = not in any recording (rule given as a decision, marked D).

Authority: this file supersedes docs/specs/timeline-spec-T-series.md where they conflict. Specifically REMOVED from the timeline: the lane-header column ("Sha..." + lock/eye/mute/up/down), the Layers/outliner column and its toggle, and the old sticky scenes panel. Schema stays at 7 (the `outliner` record from T-ALL P5 is kept and reused by section 4); no schema change is authorised in this series.

## 1. Shell layout (owner PDF pages 1-3, Clipchamp screenshots)
1. Regions, left to right: left rail | left panel | stage (canvas + stage footer) | right panel | right rail. Below the stage: the timeline card. Every region is a separate card on a darker page background; between regions is an 8 px gutter showing the page colour (the "black strip" the owner asked for). No two neighbouring regions share a background colour.
2. Dark tokens (Clipchamp measured, adapt names to tokens.css; light theme gets equally distinct tones): page/gutter #080710; rail #0b0a12 (rail and panel must differ); panel card #13121b with 12 px radius; stage background #0d0c15; timeline card #1d1d29; lane area #11111b; border 1 px OPAQUE #2a2935 (not low-opacity white). Selected/accent purple #872dfc. A unit test checks that adjacent regions differ in luminance and that no region uses the page colour except gutters.
3. Right side appears ONLY when something is selected. Nothing selected (including empty project): no right rail, no right panel, no reserved column; the stage uses the full width and the canvas stays centred. Selecting a clip or layer: right rail and panel animate in (width + fade, ~240 ms; reduced-motion = instant). Deselecting: they animate out completely. No empty dark column may remain at any moment (bug PDF p1 pink box; video B16/B1). Selecting a transition marker also opens the right side (section 6).
4. The panel collapse button lives inside each panel header (right edge). The right panel collapsed = only the right rail remains while a selection exists. Remove panel toggles from the top bar (already requested in spec T section 7).
5. Right rail: 65 px wide, icon 24 px + 10 px label under it (labels may wrap to two lines), pitch ~62 px, active item has a 56x58 rounded box #13121b and a 3 px purple indicator on the far right edge; tooltip with the name to the LEFT of the icon after ~0.5 s. Panel card 300 px wide (replaces today's 252), header title 15 px semibold + a chip with the selection type icon and count, collapse icon at the right, 12 px side padding, thin scrollbar (4 px, #737279).
6. First load: left panel = Media (not Scene), canvas white, empty-state illustration in the Media panel (spec T section 7 stays).
7. The stage footer (scenes button, size/fps, zoom controls) and the timeline card have their own tones from item 2, so playback toolbar, scenes strip and stage footer never look like one panel (PDF p3 yellow box).

## 2. Timeline (Clipchamp recording, all values at 1920x1080, +/-2 px)
Panel geometry. Let P = the panel's top y.
- Toolbar row: centre P+21, height 42. Ruler band: P+42..P+65 (24 px). Scroll area: P+68 to 4 px above the panel bottom; horizontal scrollbar 6 px high at the very bottom, vertical scrollbar 6 px wide overlay on the right, both #757580, no visible track, shown only on overflow.
- Lane heights: video/image lane 52 px, text/shape lane 28 px, audio lane 36 px (D, not measured); pitch = lane height + 8 px gap. Lane background #11111b; gap shows the panel colour #1d1d29. 23 px padding above the first and below the last lane. Text/shape clip box 30 px (1 px border), corner radius ~6 px, label bold 11-12 px white with a type icon; video clip = filmstrip + speaker chip at lower left.
- Clip colours: text #ae25b5 to #8e0090 gradient (border #490054); shape #2677a3 to #00517e (border #00203a); selected = 2 px #7b2ae7 outline + 16 px purple end-handle pills with a white 2x14 bar, label shifted ~10 px; hover = 2 px #a89cc0 outline + lighter handles.
- Playhead: 3 px white line over everything (never behind a lane, header or ghost lane) with a white shield head ~18x22 on the ruler; does not scroll vertically. Hover over ruler/lanes: 1 px grey hairline (exactly at the pointer x, not offset) + black time chip on the ruler.
- Selected clip: purple ruler band with a duration chip, following drags in real time.

Resize (video 1:10-1:40, 4:17-5:20), applied EVERY FRAME while dragging, no transition, no need for a click:
- Handle = top edge of the panel; hover/drag shows a 2 px #a864fa line and ns-resize cursor; the panel top follows the pointer 1:1.
- Max height: panel top clamps at 374 (height ~657 at 1080p; scale as ~60% of window height). Min: collapsed ~41 px.
- Centring: h = sum of lane heights + 8 px gaps. H = scroll-area height. If h + 46 <= H: lane group is vertically centred, no vertical scrollbar; first lane top = scrollTop0 + (H - h)/2. Else: group top-aligned at scrollTop0 + 23 and the area scrolls vertically with the wheel; ruler and toolbar stay pinned and lanes are clipped at the ruler line. Measured: with 2 text lanes + video the group is centred at P=712; the 4th lane triggers scrolling there.
- Ghost lanes ("Add text" above / "Add audio" below, only when exactly one real lane) count in h so centring is correct.
- Layout is recomputed from a ResizeObserver AND on every pointermove of the resize drag, never only on click (bug B3).
- Collapsed (height <= ~180): transport uses the slider layout: sparkle, skip-start, back 5 s, forward 5 s, play (white 32 px circle), "0:05.00 / 0:12.71", a horizontal seek slider (x 730-1460), fit icon. No ruler, no lanes. Above ~180 px: centred transport (skip, back 5, forward 5, play, time) with sparkle and scissors at the left, zoom pill bottom-right (130x34, zoom out, zoom in, fit width, fit window). Never show a ruler touching the scrollbar with no lanes (bug B7).
- Preview above: min preview height 160 px (timeline max height respects it); Fit is computed once per frame from the stage size, monotonic while dragging, never flipping between values (bug B5: 55, 23, 17, 26, 17...). Resizing never changes the user's zoom unexpectedly.

Panel drag detents (owner, watched live in Clipchamp; Clipchamp video 1:13-1:27 and 4:50-4:59 confirm the collapsed end state; exact thresholds are D):
- Dragging UP is free: the panel follows the pointer continuously up to the max clamp, with lanes re-centring each frame (above).
- Dragging DOWN has three zones. (1) Free zone from max height down to the DEFAULT expanded height (the height where ruler + all lanes of the current project fit; at least ruler + one video lane, ~186 px at 1080p): moves freely. (2) Detent: within ~40 px above and below the default height the panel SNAPS to the default height (a visible stop, like Clipchamp); dragging further past it by ~40 px releases the snap and the panel follows the pointer again for a short travel of ~60 px (lanes clipped from the bottom is NOT allowed to look half-cut: below the default height the lane area scrolls/clips only under the pinned ruler, never leaving a half lane under the toolbar as in our bug). (3) Collapse: past that travel (~100 px below the default) the panel jumps to the collapsed player-only layout (~41 px: transport row with seek slider only, no ruler, no lanes); there is NO state in between where lanes show as a half line. Dragging back up leaves the collapsed state with hysteresis (~60 px) and passes through the same detent. Releasing in any zone rests there; collapsed stays collapsed.
- Our current bug (owner): at max-down the playback panel and only half of one lane line remain visible. The collapsed layout replaces that state entirely. The bottom clamp is the collapsed height, never a partial layout.
- Test: drag the edge in 8 px steps from max to bottom and back; assert at every step the layout is one of {free, snapped-default, collapsed}, that lanes are re-centred within 2 px at each step, and that no step shows a clipped half lane under the toolbar or the preview shrinks below the minimum.

Lane group rules, ghost lanes, empty state, snapping, gap delete, drop rules: unchanged from spec T sections 1-2 (untyped lanes, groups V/T/A, new lane above/between/below, "+" insertion line follows the pointer, red blocked cursor, hatched gap with "Delete this gap", Replace overlay). Empty state: one dark lane spanning the whole width starting at the timeline origin, dashed box on its left ~50% centred vertically in the lane with the filmstrip illustration and "Drag & drop media here"; nothing starts mid-lane (PDF p1); no ruler/playhead.

Scroll and paging: spec T sections 4-5 unchanged. Fix B12: ruler, playhead, clips and gaps all derive from the same scrollLeft in the same frame (no frame where ruler moved and clips did not; the playhead at 0:00 must sit exactly on the clip start of a clip that starts at 0).

## 2b. Frame range row (Current / Start / End) (owner bugs, video B14 plus PDF)
- The three fields hold real values from the start (Current 0, Start 0, End = project end, 300 in the owner's project). The timeline must dim everything OUTSIDE [Start, End] (ruler AND every lane/clip area) from the first frame using these default values; today nothing is dimmed until a digit is edited (bug).
- The dim is derived live from the effective Start/End on EVERY change, not on commit: typing each digit, mouse-wheel over the field, the step arrows, dragging the field, undo/redo, project length change. It must never wait for blur/Enter (bug: wheel changes did not update the dim; it updated only after a typed value was committed). An intermediate invalid text (empty, Start > End) shows no dim change and no error until valid; wheel steps clamp (Start <= End, Current free).
- End follows the project end while the user has not set it (auto), and keeps the user's value once edited (spec T section 9 rules stay: first/last buttons jump to Start/End, play loops in range, End value survives adding clips). Fields are wide enough for 5 digits.
- Proof: e2e at default values asserts the dimmed region begins exactly at End (and ends at Start), changes while typing a digit and while wheel-scrolling, with no blur.

## 3. Selection, deselection, marquee (owner complaint, bugs B1/B2)
Root cause to remove: empty regions are not pointer targets. The lane area must have ONE background surface that fills the whole scroll content (min-height 100% of the scroll area, full scrollWidth) and receives pointerdown, so every pixel that is not a clip, a handle, a "+" line, a gap button or the ruler is "empty".
- Click (movement < 4 px) on empty space anywhere in the lane area: lanes' blank space right of/between clips, gaps between lanes, padding above/below, area below the last lane: clears the selection (clips, keyframes, outliner rows) AND, as in Clipchamp (SEEN 1:56.4), moves the playhead to the click x. Click on the ruler: seek + clear (SEEN 1:49.7). Click on a clip: select only, playhead stays (SEEN 1:55). Click on the empty canvas background or outside the stage: clear (SEEN). The right side then closes (section 1.3).
- Marquee (D, Clipchamp does not show one; owner requires it): pointerdown on empty space and move >= 4 px draws a translucent purple rectangle (fill rgba(135,45,252,.18), 1 px #872dfc border) in scroll-content coordinates, so it keeps working while the lanes scroll; clips intersecting it are selected live; release commits one selection. Plain marquee replaces the selection; Shift/Ctrl adds. An empty marquee clears. It starts from any empty point (including the 23 px padding, between lanes and below the last lane), never from the ruler (which scrubs), and edge auto-scroll while dragging near the lane-area edge. Pointer capture on the surface so releasing outside the window still ends it.
- Shift/Ctrl click toggles a clip in the selection. Esc clears. No stray purple focus border around the timeline (bug 13 of spec T).
- Selection stays in sync with canvas and outliner both ways (spec T section 12-13 reveal-in-timeline stays, now triggered from the left outliner).

## 4. Left "Scene" category = Blender outliner (Blender 3.3 recording; owner: build it HERE, not in the timeline)
The Scene category's panel today lists the scene's layers in canvas order, read-only. Replace it with the outliner below. It reuses the schema-7 `outliner` data from T-ALL P5 and the existing commands; nothing here may change the canvas, stacking, transforms, lanes, timing or export (organisation only). All behaviours are SEEN in the Blender recording unless marked D (decision from Blender documentation, not shown).

Tree. Root row = the scene (icon + scene name, no arrow, no columns, always first, cannot collapse). Children of any collection: its child collections first (creation order), then its layers (canvas front-first order, D-043). Layers directly under the root come after all collections. Empty collection = no arrow. Indent 20 px per level, a 1 px lighter guide line under each parent's arrow. Row = [indent][arrow 10 px][type icon 16 px][name][faint strip of child-type icons when collapsed (SEEN)] ... [eye][lock][mute for audio/video]. Eye/lock show on hover and while active; a hidden/locked/muted row keeps a persistent dimmed icon. Row height 24 px (Blender 20; 24 for touch), zebra rows. A collection's eye/lock/mute applies to all its layers in one undo step (shortcut, not a group). Expand state per row, remembered, transient (not in history); a new child auto-expands its parent (SEEN).

Header: title "Scene", search field (filters rows by name, keeps parents of matches), a "New collection" button (box with +) and the panel collapse button. 

Create. Header button and context menu "New" on a collection create a collection INSIDE the right-clicked/active collection (first child); on the root row, "New collection" appends at the root bottom (SEEN). Names: root level "Collection n"; child = parent name + " " + n ("New 1", "Second 1 1") (SEEN). A new collection is NOT in rename mode (SEEN).

Rename. Double-click the name (and F2, D) opens an inline field spanning the name width, text fully selected, typing replaces; Enter confirms, Esc cancels, blur confirms (D); empty name reverts. Existing children keep their names (SEEN).

Drag and drop. Press-move on a row name starts a drag (selects it). Ghost = type icon + name, or "N objects" for several selected (SEEN). Tooltip near the cursor: "Move into collection" (Blender's Ctrl-link/Shift-parent are not supported). Only collection rows and the root row are drop targets; the hovered target row turns dark #333 full width; NO insertion line (SEEN). Drop on a collection moves the dragged rows into it and expands it (collapsed targets stay collapsed and show the new icon in their strip, SEEN). Dropping on the current parent changes nothing. Collections can be dragged onto other collections/root to nest (D); never into themselves or their descendants. Multi-drag moves all selected rows (SEEN). Moves never change layer data other than collection membership (SEEN: transforms unchanged).

Delete (menu Delete, X or Delete key while the outliner has focus/hover, no confirmation, one undo step). Deleting a collection NEVER deletes layers: its layers and sub-collections move up to the deleted collection's parent (SEEN twice). Menu also has "Delete hierarchy" (SEEN in menu, D semantics): deletes the collection with all layers inside it via the normal layer-delete path, one undo step, undoable. Deleting selected layer rows deletes those layers (normal delete).

Copy/paste/duplicate (menu items SEEN, behaviour D from Blender docs). "Duplicate collection" = a new sibling right after it containing duplicates of all its layers (new ids, same timing, tracks via the usual clip rules), name "<name>.001"; sub-collections duplicate too. Ctrl+C copies the selected rows (layers or collections with contents) into the in-app clipboard, Ctrl+V pastes into the active collection (or root); shortcuts apply only while the outliner has focus, so canvas Ctrl+C/V are unchanged. A pasted/duplicated layer follows the existing duplicate-layer naming. One undo step each.

Selection. Click selects the row and the layer on canvas and in the timeline (reveal-in-timeline, spec T 13); selected rows get a lighter row, the active one accent text; a collection row click selects all layers inside it (D) and makes it the active collection. Ctrl toggles, Shift selects a range (D). Rubber-band: press-drag on empty outliner space draws a dashed rectangle and selects the touched layer rows (SEEN). Selecting on canvas/timeline highlights the row, expands its parents and scrolls the outliner to it.

Context menus (items SEEN 0:04 and 0:23). Root: New collection, Paste. Collection: New, Duplicate collection, Copy, Paste, -, Delete, Delete hierarchy, -, Select objects, Deselect objects, -, Visibility > (Hide all, Show all), Lock all, Unlock all. Layer: Rename, Duplicate, Copy, Delete, Show in timeline. Menu style: Blender-like dark panel, 20 px items, hover orange-ish accent #c8512a... use the app's menu component styled with outliner tokens; disabled items grey with reason.

Look (outliner only, same in both themes): row zebra #474747/#4f4f4f, selected #575757, active #666666, drag-target #333333, text #bcbcbc, active text yellow #e6d800, selected-not-active blue #38a0d6, icons: collection = light archive box, layer icons orange, 16 px. 

Removed with this change: outliner column and its toggle button in the timeline, lane headers, "Layers" header. Functions of the lane headers move to: the outliner (eye/lock/mute per layer), the timeline right-click menu (Lock/Hide/Mute track and clip, Move lane up/down), Alt+Up/Down (existing D-035) and the clip toolbar. Tests that used the header buttons are rewritten to these paths; coverage is not dropped.

## 6. Transitions (Clipchamp transitions recording; hover-animation and drag parts are D)
- Adding: hovering within ~20 px of a cut between two ADJACENT clips on the same lane shows a 14 px purple rounded "+" at the cut centre with tooltip "Add transition" (~0.25 s). - Marker right-click: Remove transition, Change duration (D). Delete key on a selected marker removes it. Undo steps one each.
