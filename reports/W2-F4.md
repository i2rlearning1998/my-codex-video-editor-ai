# Report: Wave 2 part F4 (W2-F4): the Draw Eraser and shared brush settings (2026-09-26)

## 1. Summary

- **The Draw panel has an Eraser**, next to Pen, Marker and Highlighter.
  - Drag it over freehand strokes: each stroke it touches disappears at once, and letting go removes them all as one undo step ("Erase").
  - It only erases drawings, never text, shapes, images or videos.
  - Its size is the panel's Size. Color and Opacity are greyed out while it is active.
- **Size, color and opacity are one shared set.**
  - Each brush still starts with its own default size and opacity (the Highlighter at 40%).
  - Once you change the size or the opacity, every brush keeps what you set.
- **Still not possible:** erasing part of a stroke. The eraser removes whole strokes, as the ledger asks.

## 6. Decisions made

- **D-079:** how the Eraser works and how the brush settings are shared.
  - It hits a stroke when it comes within its radius plus half the stroke's width.
  - It skips strokes on locked tracks.
  - Touched strokes are hidden while dragging, and nothing is committed until release.
  - Brush defaults apply until the user changes the size or opacity.

## 7. Not tested, known gaps, risks

- **Whole strokes only:** there is no partial erase, and no eraser outline under the pointer (the cursor changes to a cell cross).
- **Not covered by a test:**
  - the locked-track skip (it is implemented in `erasableStrokes`);
  - erasing a drawing inside a group (it is deleted from the group);
  - strokes with an animation preset mid-slide (they are erased where they are drawn).
- **Not tested:** very long strokes (up to 5,000 points) against a fast eraser, which is a performance question; touch or pen input.
