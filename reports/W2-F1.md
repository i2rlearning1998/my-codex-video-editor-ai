# Report: Wave 2 part F1 (W2-F1): selection core, capability menus, multi-selection box, Ungroup and layer order (2026-09-26)

## 1. Summary

This is the first part of the Canva-parity series (spec `docs/specs/CANVA-PARITY-SPEC.md`).

- **Multi-selection is now visible.** Selecting two or more objects draws one dashed box around them all and keeps each object's own outline. A small action bar above the box offers Group, Duplicate, Delete and More (the full right-click menu). A single group gets Ungroup there instead.
- **Menus offer only what every selected item supports.** Speed, Reverse and Freeze frame now appear only for video and audio clips, on the canvas and the timeline. They never appear when an image, shape or text is selected (spec bug 2).
- **Ungroup** is in the right-click menu, the action bar and on Ctrl+Shift+G. A group that was moved, rotated or faded ungroups without anything moving on the canvas. When that is impossible, Ungroup is greyed out and says why.
- **Layer order.** A new Layer submenu offers Bring to front, Bring forward, Send backward and Send to back; they are also in the palette, and Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[ step forward and backward.
- **The duplicate-import report did not reproduce** here. I added a guard and a regression test (details in section 7).
- **Architecture:** one selection system that the menus, the action bar and, later, the timeline clip context share (D-075).

## 6. Decisions made

- D-073: Speed, Reverse and Freeze frame are for video and audio clips only.
- D-074: Ungroup bakes the group's transform into its children; the frozen core rule is unchanged.
- D-075: the shared selection, menu and action-bar architecture; the LCR adding CV-040, CV-041, CV-042 and SHP-020.
- D-076: the duplicate-import report did not reproduce; guard and regression test.

## 7. Not tested, known gaps, risks

- **Duplicate import (spec bug 6).** In the sandbox Chromium, dragging a card, by a synthetic drag and by a real mouse drag, added one layer and no asset or stored file. Storage held one media file and one thumbnail before and after. I could not reproduce the report, so the fix is a guard: a card drop is always treated as a reference, even if the browser also attaches file data. If it still happens in your Chrome, please send the exact steps (which card, where it was dropped, and what you saw duplicated).
- **The dashed box has no handles yet.** Resizing and rotating the whole selection is W2-F2 (contract revision 6). Moving several objects together already worked and is unchanged.
- **Lock and Hide** stay greyed out in the menu (CV-024 and LYR-006); CV-020 therefore stays Todo.
- **The timeline menu** follows the new rules but still draws its own buttons; moving it onto the shared renderer comes with the timeline clip context (backlog).
- **Spec cluster items** Refresh and Comment are not built (backlog).
- **Not tested:** right-to-left layout of the action bar; the action bar at high canvas zoom near the stage edges (it is clamped to the stage); Ungroup of a group nested inside another group from the canvas (covered by the same code path, not by an e2e test).

## 8. Architecture and contract impact

- **Schema:** unchanged (5). **Dependencies:** none. **Contracts:** unchanged; the core `UNGROUP` identity rule is kept.
- **New files:**
  - `src/ui/selection-context.ts` (selection items and capabilities);
  - `src/ui/context-menu.ts` (the shared menu renderer) and `src/ui/canvas-menu.ts` (canvas entries);
  - `src/ui/selection-actions.ts` (the action bar);
  - `src/ui/ungroup.ts` and `src/ui/arrange.ts`;
  - `e2e/selection-w2f.spec.ts` and `tests/selection-w2f.test.ts`.
- **Changed:**
  - `contextActions` is built on the capabilities, so the canvas menu, the timeline menu, the palette and the shortcuts all follow the same rule;
  - the speed commands require video or audio clips;
  - the renderer draws the dashed multi-selection box (`multiSelectionBox`);
  - new commands: `ungroup` (Ctrl+Shift+G) and `arrange-front`, `arrange-forward` (Ctrl+]), `arrange-backward` (Ctrl+[) and `arrange-back`;
  - the window's file-drop import ignores drags that carry the in-app asset type.
