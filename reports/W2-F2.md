# Report: Wave 2 part F2 (W2-F2): resizing and rotating a multi-selection, and the oversized-scaling bug (2026-09-26)

## 1. Summary

- **The multi-selection box now works like a single object's box.**
  - Its corners resize every selected object together, from the opposite corner, or from the center with Alt.
  - Its side handles stretch them along one axis. They only appear when every selected object is straight and none is text, because text or rotated objects would be distorted.
  - The round handle rotates the whole selection about its center.
  - Each drag is one undo step: "Resize layers" or "Rotate layers".
- **Fixed: the oversized-scaling bug (spec bug 5).** It reproduced. Dragging a corner of a wide or tall object made it grow faster than the pointer, because the old rule followed whichever side changed more in relative terms. The corner now follows the pointer, as in Canva.
- Both behaviours are written into the interaction contract as revision 6 (D-077).

## 6. Decisions made

- D-077: interaction contract revision 6 (projected corner multiplier; the multi-selection box), the shared `decomposeMatrix`, side handles only for straight non-text selections, and no snapping when resizing or rotating the box.

## 7. Not tested, known gaps, risks

- **The corner rule changed for every object.** It is still proportional, but the size now follows the pointer's projection onto the diagonal rather than the larger relative change. Anyone used to the old feel will notice that wide objects grow more slowly on diagonal drags. That is the intended fix.
- **Resizing or rotating the box does not snap**; moving it still snaps. Shift-rotation steps (CV-010) are not built.
- **A layer inside a non-uniformly scaled group** cannot be rotated or stretched with others: the gesture is cancelled with a message. This case is not covered by an e2e test.
- **Not tested:** very large selections (performance); the handles at extreme zoom; right-to-left layout.

## 8. Architecture and contract impact

- **Contracts:** `TRANSFORM_INTERACTION_CONTRACT.md` revision 6; `TRANSFORM_CONTRACT.md` gets a pointer note only.
- **Schema:** unchanged (5). **Dependencies:** none.
- **Core:** additive `decomposeMatrix`, which Ungroup now also uses. `resizeTransform`'s proportional rule uses the projection.
- **Render:**
  - `multiSelectionFrame`, `multiSelectionGeometry`, `multiSelectionStretchable` and `hitMultiHandle`;
  - `RenderSource.selectionFrame` (the transformed frame during a gesture);
  - the canvas draws the box's handles.
- **UI:** `TransformInteraction` has a multi-selection gesture (`multiAffine`), and the canvas pointer code hit-tests the box's handles.
