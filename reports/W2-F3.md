# Report: Wave 2 part F3 (W2-F3): the Position panel (2026-09-26)

## 1. Summary

- **A new Position button opens a panel with two tabs.** The button is on the toolbar for every kind of object, and on the small action bar that appears when several objects are selected.
  - **Arrange:** Bring to front, Bring forward, Send backward and Send to back; the six align buttons; Distribute horizontally and vertically; and Relative to canvas.
  - **Layers:** every layer as a flat list, frontmost first, each with its type icon. Groups show a folder and their number of items.
- **Using the list:** click a row to select it, Shift+click to add, and drag a row onto another to move it to that place in the stacking order.
- **Each action is one undo step.** They use the same commands as the right-click menu and the shortcuts.
- **Still not possible:** dragging a layer into or out of a group (LYR-004 stays Todo).

## 6. Decisions made

- **D-078:**
  - The panel's design: it is shared with the Animate panel's frame and reuses the existing commands.
  - The Layers tab lists top-level layers only, and a drop moves the dragged layer to the target row's place.
  - Moving layers into or out of groups is not built.

## 7. Not tested, known gaps, risks

- **The list is flat and top-level only.** You cannot reach a group's children from it (the Scene list still shows the tree), and you cannot drag into or out of a group (LYR-004).
- **The panel closes on any click outside it,** including the top-bar Undo button, as the Animate panel does. Ctrl+Z keeps it open.
- **The panel covers part of the canvas's upper half** while it is open, as the Animate panel does.
- **Not tested:** keyboard-only reordering (there is none; rows are keyboard-selectable only); very long layer lists (the list scrolls at 320 px, untested); Hindi layout of the panel; right-to-left layout.
