# Brief W2-F4: the Draw Eraser and shared brush settings

Wave: 2 (continuation), fourth part of the W2-F series. Base: PR #9 head (W2-F3). End tag: `w2-f4` (created at merge). Branch: `claude/wave-2f4-draw-eraser` (stacked on PR #9).
Authority: `AGENTS.md`, then this brief, then `docs/FEATURES.md`. Reference: `docs/specs/CANVA-PARITY-SPEC.md` section 7 and section 9 (item 6).

## 1. Goal

1. The Draw panel gets an Eraser that removes whole freehand strokes it touches, as one undo step per drag.
2. Size, color and opacity are one set of controls shared by Pen, Marker and Highlighter.

## 2. In scope

- SHP-020 (added by the W2-F1 LCR).

## 3. Out of scope

- A partial (pixel) eraser, and Magic Eraser (AI, Wave 10).
- Erasing anything that is not a freehand stroke.
- Shape editing (W5-D) and text styling (W2-F5).

## 4. Ledger Change Requests

None.

## 5. Contracts, schema and dependencies

- No contract change: draw mode already bypasses picking (interaction contract revision 5), and the Eraser is a draw mode.
- Schema stays 5: stored drawings keep their `brush` of pen, marker or highlighter.
- No dependency is added.

## 6. Design notes

- **`DrawMode = Brush | 'eraser'`** in `render/drawing.ts`. The session's draw mode uses it.
- **`DrawTool`:**
  - collects the touched strokes during the drag (`erasableStrokes`, `strokesTouched`);
  - the shell hides them from the render source (`withoutLayers`);
  - `finish` commits one "Erase" transaction.
- **Hit rule:** the distance from the sampled pointer path to the stroke's polyline in world space must be at most the eraser radius plus half the drawn stroke width. Locked tracks are skipped.
- **Shared settings:** brush defaults apply on picking a brush only until the user changes the size or opacity.

## 7. Required tests

- e2e `[SHP-020]`:
  - the Marker keeps the Pen's size and color;
  - the Eraser disables color and opacity;
  - a drag across two of three strokes hides them before release with no commit;
  - release commits one "Erase" step that removes both layers and their clips, while the example's own layers stay;
  - one undo restores both;
  - a drag that touches nothing adds no history.
- Unit `[SHP-020]`: the hit threshold, fast-move sampling, the end cap, and the shared-settings rule.
