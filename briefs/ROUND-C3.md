## THE PRINCIPLE (owner, authoritative; read twice)

The WORK of the in-panel Director and of Claude through the MCP is the same: the person ends with a finished video in the same system (same Project, Videos, Style, memory, credits). The WAY is different.

- The Director is OUR model. It is bound by the owner's rules (it may only offer Studios, prompts, upload and library for assets; it never goes to other platforms; refusals are enforced).
- Claude is the PERSON'S model, on the person's own plan, with its own tools and connectors. The owner cannot bind it. For Claude the rulebook, playbook and skill are GUIDANCE (how this tool works and how to make a good video here), with flexibility. The only hard limits are what the MCP itself enforces: security, credits and limits, Admin tool switches, the emergency switch, and never showing the plan or project structure.
- The MCP is hands and real-time data: it tells Claude live what exists (Styles, Studios and their fields and costs, enabled providers, credits, the library, what changed in the tool), collects what Claude sends, builds layers from it, exports, and returns the result. Claude then checks the result and, if it is not what the person asked, writes the code again and sends it again.
- Whatever the editor can make must be reachable by Claude: code layers (Tier A, B, C) and every editing capability. What code cannot make comes from assets: the person provides it, the platform's Studios make it, the person gets prompts to make it on any platform, it comes from the library, or Claude or the person's other tools make it. The person chooses; nothing is forced.
- A video made through Claude must look exactly as if it was made in our tool: a Project, its Videos, saved in the cloud, opening in the editor and in AI mode, and its graphics and compositions saved in the Project's AI library like the ones imported into the editor's AI category.
