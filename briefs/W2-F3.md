## 1. Goal

A Position button on the context toolbar opens a panel with two tabs:

- **Arrange:** layer order, align and distribute as buttons.
- **Layers:** the composition's layers as a flat list that reorders by drag. Groups show a folder icon.

The panel is keyed off the selection, like the W2-F1 menus, so multi-selections reach it from the action cluster.

## 6. Design notes

- `src/ui/position-panel.ts` follows the Animate panel pattern: a dialog in the canvas stage, tabs, closed by an outside click or Escape.
- Arrange reuses the existing commands: `arrangeSelection` and `canArrange` (W2-F1), and `alignSelection`, `distributeSelection` and `canDistribute` (W2-D). So the undo labels and the rules match the menu, palette and shortcuts.
- Layers drag uses HTML5 drag and drop. A drop is one `MOVE_LAYER` to the target row's index ("Reorder layer").
- The entry points are a `position` control in every toolbar layout and a `position` button in the selection action cluster.
