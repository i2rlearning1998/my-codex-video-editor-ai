# Brief W2-F5: text styling from the toolbar

Wave: 2 (continuation), fifth part of the W2-F series. Base: PR #10 head (W2-F4). End tag: `w2-f5` (created at merge). Branch: `claude/wave-2f5-text-styling` (stacked on PR #10).
Authority: `AGENTS.md`, then this brief, then `docs/FEATURES.md`. Reference: `docs/specs/CANVA-PARITY-SPEC.md` section 9 (item 3).

## 1. Goal

The text toolbar's Font, Weight, Align and Spacing controls stop being greyed out and edit the selected text layer:

- **Font:** system fonts.
- **Weight:** plus Italic.
- **Align:** left, center, right and justify.
- **Spacing:** line height, letter spacing, paragraph spacing, and case.

The owner chose this styling subset now; the font picker, font loading and uploads stay in W3.

## 6. Design notes

- `render/text-style.ts` holds the style model, a defensive reader, the font shorthand, case and measuring.
- The layout gains line height, paragraph spacing and paragraph ends.
- The renderer draws alignment, justify and letter spacing. The preview and the export worker share it.
- The toolbar gets `textStyleCommands`: it validates each value, and grows the box height in the same step when an unwrapped box overflows.

## 7. Required tests

- e2e `[CV-037][TXT-014]`: the pixel positions for right, center and justified text.
- e2e `[TXT-016]`: the measured line, paragraph and letter offsets, and a refused out-of-range value.
- e2e `[TXT-017]`: upper, title and lower case change the drawn width, while the typed text is kept.
- e2e `[TXT-010]`: font, weight and italic change the drawing, one undo step each.
- Unit tests: the defaults, the fallbacks, the layout offsets, case, and height growth.
